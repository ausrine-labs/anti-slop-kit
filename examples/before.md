happened next at our company changed everything about how we think
about documentation.

our own internal wiki and found something surprising. Meadowlark isn't
just a documentation tool — it's a living tapestry of your team's
collective intelligence.

Our seamless, robust platform leverages cutting-edge retrieval to
unlock the full potential of your existing docs. It's a testament to
what a small team can build when they delve into a problem with
meticulous attention, careful curation, and genuine passion.

Users report stunning results. One customer said the onboarding
experience was incredible. Another told us it was a game-changer for
