# Meadowlark: search that reads your team's old answers

Support tickets at Verso repeated themselves 41% of the time. The same
eleven questions, asked by new people, answered from scratch by whoever
was on rotation. The answers already existed — in a wiki nobody
searched, in Slack threads nobody could find.

Meadowlark indexes what a team has already written and answers from it.
It reads Notion, Google Docs, Slack, and a folder of Markdown files,
then returns the passage and the link, not a summary.

Verso ran it for six weeks. Repeat tickets fell to 12%. Onboarding
questions that used to route to a senior engineer now resolve in the
search box; the engineer got roughly four hours of her week back.

Pricing is $12 per seat per month. Indexing takes about ten minutes for
a 5,000-document workspace. There is no free tier and no sales call —
the trial is fourteen days and it starts when you connect the first
source.

One caveat worth stating plainly: Meadowlark is only as good as what
your team wrote down. Teams whose knowledge lives entirely in people's
heads get a fast search over an empty room.
